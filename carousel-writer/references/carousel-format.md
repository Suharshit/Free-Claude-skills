# Carousel format

## Answer layout

Use this layout in the chat. Replace every `[..]`; keep the headings.

```markdown
## 3 slide-1 options

| # | Formula | Headline (≤ 10 words) | Sub-line (≤ 8 words) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 ⭐ | [formula name] | [..] | [..] |
| 2 | [formula name] | [..] | [..] |
| 3 | [formula name] | [..] | [..] |

**Recommended: 1.** [one-line reason]

## Carousel: [structure] · [N] slides

| # | Headline | Body | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [recommended headline] | [sub-line] | [visual note] |
| 2 | [..] | [≤ 25 words] | [..] |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |
| [N] | [CTA headline] | [one line, keyword in CAPITALS] | Text only |

## Caption
[Hook line that works on its own]
[2-4 short lines: what's inside, who it's for]

[One CTA line]

[#tag1 #tag2 #tag3 (#tag4 #tag5)]

## Fill before posting
- [ ] [bracket] on slide [n]: [what to measure or confirm], or cut it.
```

Then the voice card (only when made or updated), then the one-line follow-up offer.

## Structures

Pick one.

| Structure | Slides | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Numbered list** | Hook (N things) → why it matters → one item per slide → CTA | Tips, tools, ideas |
| **Step by step** | Hook (outcome) → what you need → step 1...n → the result → CTA | Tutorials, recipes, setups |
| **Myth vs truth** | Hook (question or myth) → myth → truth, repeated → the simple version → CTA | Correcting assumptions |
| **What it is / isn't** | Hook → it isn't... → it is... → an example → where it helps → CTA | Explaining a concept |
| **Before / after** | Hook (the change) → the old way → what changed → the new way → the result → CTA | Real improvements with proof |
| **Mistakes** | Hook (N mistakes) → mistake + fix, one per slide → the quick checklist → CTA | Fixing common errors |

## Slide rules

- **Slide count:** 6-8 by default (the user or the voice card can change it).
- **Slide 1:** the promise. Biggest text in the carousel. See `slide-hooks.md`.
- **Slide 2:** why it matters, or the problem in the reader's words. It earns the swipe; never repeat slide 1.
- **Content slides:** one point each. The headline states the point; the body gives the how (a step, an example, the exact words to use). All content headlines follow one pattern, e.g. all numbered "1 / ..." or all starting with a verb.
- **Last slide:** the one CTA. A keyword goes in CAPITALS.
- **Body:** 25 words or fewer per slide. If a point needs more, it needs two slides.

## Visual notes

One short note per slide, so the creator or a designer knows what goes on it. Use one of:

- **Text only**
- **Photo of [what]** (the creator's own photo or footage still)
- **Screenshot of [what]**
- **Simple diagram: [A → B → C]**, written out with its labels
- **Checklist box: [items]**
- **Before / after split: [left] | [right]**

Name the exact thing to show ("photo of the bubbly starter in the jar"), never "relevant image". No stock-photo notes.

## Caption rules

- **Line 1 works alone:** it shows in the feed before "more". Don't just repeat slide 1; give a reason to open it.
- **2-4 short lines** after it: what's inside and who it's for, in the creator's voice.
- **One CTA line**, the same CTA as the last slide.
- **3-5 hashtags:** topic, audience and task tags. No generic or spammy tags (#viral, #explorepage, #fyp).
- Short lines, no walls of text, no emoji runs. Emojis only if the voice card shows the creator uses them.

## Checks (fix before answering)

- [ ] 3 slide-1 options, 3 different formulas, each within the length limits; one recommended with a reason.
- [ ] Slide 1 makes sense alone and promises exactly what the slides deliver.
- [ ] Slide 2 earns the swipe and doesn't repeat slide 1.
- [ ] One point per content slide; every content slide has something to act on or keep.
- [ ] Every headline ≤ 10 words; every body ≤ 25 words; content headlines follow one pattern.
- [ ] Slide count within 6-8 (or what the user asked for).
- [ ] Every slide has a specific visual note.
- [ ] Sounds like the voice card: their words, their analogies, their order of thought. Nothing fancier than the samples.
- [ ] None of the banned hype or filler words.
- [ ] Every number is from the user, a count of the slides, or in a `[bracket]`; every bracket is listed under "Fill before posting".
- [ ] No income, follower or "go viral" promises.
- [ ] Exactly one CTA: last slide plus the caption's CTA line; keyword (if any) in CAPITALS.
- [ ] Caption: standalone first line, 2-4 short lines, one CTA, 3-5 relevant hashtags.
