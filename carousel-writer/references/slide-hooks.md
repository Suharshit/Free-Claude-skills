# Slide-1 hooks

Slide 1 decides whether anyone swipes. It is a headline (10 words or fewer) and an optional sub-line (8 words or fewer). Write 3 options, each from a different formula, and recommend one.

If the voice card lists the creator's own hook formulas, use those first.

## Formulas

Fill the brackets with the topic. Numbers must be real (given by the user, or a count of the slides themselves) or stay in brackets.

| # | Formula | Pattern | Works best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Number promise | "[N] [things] for [outcome]" | Tips, tools, checklists |
| 2 | How to, in steps | "How to [outcome] in [N] steps" | Tutorials, setups, recipes |
| 3 | Myth | "You don't need [thing people assume] to [outcome]" | Beginners who think it's harder than it is |
| 4 | Mistakes | "[N] mistakes that [bad result]" | Fixing what most people get wrong |
| 5 | Before / after | "[Task]: [X] before, [Y] now" | A real, measured improvement |
| 6 | What it actually is | "What [thing] actually is (and isn't)" | Explaining a concept simply |
| 7 | Copy this | "Steal my [template / checklist / setup] for [task]" | Something readers can copy directly |
| 8 | Question | "Why does your [thing] keep [problem]?" | A common frustration with a clear answer |

## Rules for every slide 1

- **Headline ≤ 10 words; sub-line ≤ 8 words.** The sub-line adds the count, the audience or the format ("No kit needed. 4 steps.").
- **Makes sense with no caption.** Someone scrolling past must know what they get.
- **Promise what the swipe delivers.** If it says 5 steps, there are 5 step slides.
- **A number when there is one.** Counts of slides inside the carousel are always real; outside numbers need the user's source.
- **Name who it's for or what they get.** "Your first sourdough starter" beats "this baking trick".
- **No clickbait** ("you won't believe slide 4"), and no income, follower or "go viral" promises.

## The user's slide-1 idea

If the user gives one, it is option 1:
- Keep their words and their idea.
- Tighten it to the length limits and fix grammar.
- Name the formula it fits closest, or "own hook" if none fits.

Options 2 and 3 then use two other formulas.

## Picking the recommended hook

Prefer, in order:
1. The one the slides fully pay off.
2. The one that promises something to keep (steps, a checklist, a template), because saved carousels are kept for later use.
3. The one that sounds most like the voice card.

Give the reason in one line.
