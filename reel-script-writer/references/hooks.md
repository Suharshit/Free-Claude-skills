# Hooks

The hook is the first 2 seconds: one spoken line plus the on-screen text. Write 3 options, each from a different formula, and recommend one.

If the voice card lists the creator's own hook formulas, use those first.

## Formulas

Fill the brackets with the topic. Numbers must be real (given by the user) or stay in brackets.

| # | Formula | Pattern | Works best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Stop doing it by hand | "Still [doing the task] the slow way?" | A faster method, a tool, a shortcut |
| 2 | Before / after | "[Task] took me [X]. Now it takes [Y]." | A real, measured improvement |
| 3 | Myth | "You don't need [thing people assume] to [outcome]." | Beginners who think it's harder than it is |
| 4 | Mistake | "Stop [common mistake] if you want [outcome]." | Fixing something most people get wrong |
| 5 | Number promise | "[N] [things] that [outcome]." | Tips, lists, quick wins |
| 6 | Behind the curtain | "Here's exactly how I [do the thing]. No edits." | Showing a real process or raw result |
| 7 | Honest fail | "I tried [X]. Here's where it went wrong." | Lessons, experiments, trust-building |
| 8 | Question | "Why does your [thing] keep [problem]?" | A common frustration with a clear answer |
| 9 | Copy this | "Steal my [template / recipe / setup] for [task]." | Something viewers can copy directly |

## Rules for every hook

- **Spoken:** 2 seconds or less, about 6 words or fewer.
- **On-screen:** 8 words or fewer. It can differ from the spoken line, but must say the same thing.
- **Show something in the first 2 seconds:** the creator's face, the result, or motion. Say which in the script's first shot.
- **Name who it's for or what they get.** "Your sourdough starter" beats "this trick".
- **Specific beats vague.** "In 5 days" beats "fast"; "3 ingredients" beats "a few things".
- **The video must pay it off.** Never promise what the script doesn't deliver: no fake shock, no "you won't believe".
- **No income, follower or "go viral" promises.**

## The user's base hook

If the user gives a base hook, it is option 1:
- Keep their words and their idea.
- Tighten it to the length limits and fix grammar.
- Name the formula it fits closest, or "own hook" if none fits.

Options 2 and 3 then use two other formulas.

## Picking the recommended hook

Prefer, in order:
1. The one the topic can actually prove on screen in the first 5 seconds.
2. The one that names the viewer's problem or outcome, not just the topic.
3. The one that sounds most like the voice card.

Give the reason in one line.
